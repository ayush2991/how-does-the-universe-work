The Publishing Philosophy / First Principles

Why Explorable Explanations?

Physics is often taught backwards: first you are handed abstract mathematical equations, and only later are you invited to guess what they physically mean.

By creating interactive simulations with active controls, scrubbable parameters, and live coordinated clocks, we allow you to manipulate the underlying geometry of nature directly. When you see how steering a vector sideways reduces its vertical component, time dilation stops being an abstract puzzle—it becomes an intuitive geometric certainty.
