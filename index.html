Why Explorable Explanations?

Physics is often taught backwards: first you are given mathematical equations, and only later are you invited to guess what they physically mean.

By creating interactive simulations with active controls, sliders, and live clocks, we allow you to play with the underlying parameters of nature directly. When you see how steering a vector sideways reduces its vertical component, time dilation stops being an abstract puzzle—it becomes an intuitive geometric certainty.
